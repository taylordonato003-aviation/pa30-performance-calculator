PA-30 Performance Calculator

Piper Twin Comanche PA-30 · Standard configuration, 3600 lb max gross weight

Simulation / training use only — not valid for flight dispatch. All figures here were manually digitized from scanned Piper PA-30 POH chart images by approximate visual curve-tracing, then fit to simple interpolation models. They are reasonable approximations, not certified performance data. Always verify against the actual, current, aircraft-specific POH before any real flight planning or dispatch decision.

Laid out the way you'd actually plan a flight: know your weight first, then when/where/how high, then the airports involved, then walk the flight itself — takeoff, climb, cruise, descent, land.

Conditions

Weight/C.G. fields are normally filled in by the Weight & Balance worksheet above; pressure altitude/OAT/wind are normally filled in by the Airports lookup above. All stay directly editable here for a quick what-if without re-touching those. Positive wind = headwind, negative = tailwind. Weights are capped at 3600 lb (standard, non tip-tank gross weight). Leave landing weight blank to use takeoff weight (e.g. a short local flight with negligible fuel burn).

Cruise Phase

Whatever route distance is left after climb and descent, at the cruise power set in Conditions — groundspeed is the route's own per-leg winds above, distance-weighted.

Descent

There's no POH chart for descent rate — it's a pilot technique choice, not a performance limit, so set your own plan above. Descent phase is cruise altitude down to destination field elevation at 55% power, headwind from the station nearest destination along the route's final course.
