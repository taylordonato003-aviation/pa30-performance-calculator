Simulation / training use only — not valid for flight dispatch. All figures here were manually digitized from scanned Piper PA-30 POH chart images by approximate visual curve-tracing, then fit to simple interpolation models. They are reasonable approximations, not certified performance data. Always verify against the actual, current, aircraft-specific POH before any real flight planning or dispatch decision.

Airports

Looking up an airport fills in its field elevation, then live METAR (when available) upgrades pressure altitude to a true altimeter-corrected value, fills in OAT (from departure), and — once you pick a runway — computes the actual headwind/crosswind component from live wind direction and speed against that runway's magnetic heading (derived from its own number, e.g. Rwy 34 ≈ 340°). Runway length/surface/weather are reference only; always verify against current charts and a real briefing, as this is a convenience lookup, not NOTAMs or an official briefing.

Conditions

Positive wind = headwind, negative = tailwind. Picking a runway above computes these automatically from live wind — editing them by hand overrides that until you change the runway or airport again. Weights are capped at 3600 lb (standard, non tip-tank gross weight). Leave landing weight blank to use takeoff weight (e.g. a short local flight with negligible fuel burn).

Go-Around / Balked Landing

Approximate reference only — not a POH chart. This aircraft's digitized performance section has no dedicated balked-landing / landing-configuration climb chart. The figures below are the clean-configuration (gear and flaps retracted) climb numbers from Fig 5-09/5-10/5-11, at your landing weight. A real go-around starts with flaps and gear still out, which costs climb performance this does not model — especially on one engine, where initial climb can be marginal or negative in that transition. Treat this as a best-case floor, not a guarantee, and fly the go-around per the actual aircraft's POH emergency procedures.

Route

—

Waypoints accept a 4-letter ICAO airport, a VOR/NDB identifier, or a 5-letter RNAV/GPS fix — type one and press Enter or Tab. If more than one navaid shares that identifier, you'll get a dropdown to pick the right one by name/country. Winds/temps aloft are a forecast product (not a live observation) from the same live-data source as METAR, interpolated to your cruise altitude from the nearest reporting station to each leg — a convenience estimate for situational awareness, not a substitute for a real weather briefing.
