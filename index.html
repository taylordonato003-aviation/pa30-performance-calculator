PA-30 Performance Calculator

Piper Twin Comanche PA-30 · Standard configuration, 3600 lb max gross weight

Simulation / training use only — not valid for flight dispatch. All figures here were manually digitized from scanned Piper PA-30 POH chart images by approximate visual curve-tracing, then fit to simple interpolation models. They are reasonable approximations, not certified performance data. Always verify against the actual, current, aircraft-specific POH before any real flight planning or dispatch decision.

Laid out the way you'd actually plan a flight: know your weight first, then when/where/how high, then the airports involved, then walk the flight itself — takeoff, climb, cruise, descent, land.
